Claude Spend Bar

Your claude.ai usage, in the menu bar. How much of your session and weekly limits you have left, without opening a browser tab.

Download for Mac

macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon · signed and notarised by Apple

Installing

  1. Open the downloaded ClaudeSpendBar.dmg.
  2. Drag Claude Spend Bar onto the Applications folder in the same window.
  3. Open your Applications folder and double-click Claude Spend Bar. Nothing appears in the Dock — look for the clay triangle in the menu bar, at the top right of your screen.

It adds itself to your login items the first time it runs, so it is there after a restart. You can turn that off in its settings.

Connecting your Claude account

The app reads your usage as you — which means it needs the session key your browser already holds. It is kept in your Mac's Keychain and sent to claude.ai and nowhere else.

  1. Click the triangle in the menu bar, then Set session key…
  2. Open claude.ai in Safari, and sign in if you are not already.
  3. Press ⌥⌘I to open the Web Inspector, then select the Network tab.
  4. Go to Settings → Usage.
  5. Type usage in the filter box, then click one of the usage requests.
  6. Open Headers, find the Cookie row, and copy the whole value.
  7. Paste it into the app. It finds the key inside and stores only that.

If the Web Inspector is missing in Safari, turn on Show features for web developers in Safari ▸ Settings ▸ Advanced.

Installing with Homebrew instead

brew install --cask tkachenkobogdan/tap/claude-spend-bar

Updates then arrive with brew upgrade. The disk image above does not update itself — download it again for a new version.
